# **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

## **Project: MarvelVerse – Interactive Marvel Cinematic Universe Explorer**

**Version:** 1.0

---

# **1\. Executive Summary**

## **Product Name**

**MarvelVerse**

---

## **One-Line Vision**

A premium, cinematic, interactive platform that allows users to explore the Marvel Cinematic Universe through movies, series, characters, timelines, relationships, and immersive storytelling.

---

## **Vision Statement**

MarvelVerse is not intended to be another movie database or fandom wiki.

Instead, it aims to become an immersive digital experience where users can explore the MCU naturally through connected content, cinematic animations, interactive timelines, and relationship graphs.

The experience should feel closer to navigating the MCU itself than browsing a website.

---

# **2\. Problem Statement**

Current MCU information is scattered across multiple platforms.

Users often need to switch between:

* Marvel Official  
* IMDb  
* Wikipedia  
* Fandom  
* YouTube  
* Reddit

to understand:

* Timeline order  
* Character appearances  
* Relationships  
* Teams  
* Story progression  
* Phase organization  
* Actor information

There is no unified, visually immersive platform that combines all of this into one interactive experience.

---

# **3\. Product Goals**

MarvelVerse should enable users to:

* Explore every MCU Phase.  
* Explore every MCU movie and series.  
* Understand the complete timeline.  
* Learn about every character.  
* View character relationships.  
* Discover actor-film connections.  
* Understand events across phases.  
* Experience cinematic interactions.  
* Search the MCU intuitively.

# **17\. Risks & Assumptions**

## **Risks**

* Copyright and licensing constraints for official Marvel assets.  
* Maintaining accurate and up-to-date MCU data.  
* Scope expansion due to the size of the MCU.  
* Performance impact from advanced animations.  
* Increased maintenance as new titles release.

## **Assumptions**

* The initial version will focus on curated MCU information.  
* The product will use a consistent design system across all pages.  
* Motion and interactions are core features, not optional enhancements.  
* Future scalability is considered from the start.

---

# **18\. Out of Scope**

The project will **not** include in Version 1:

* Movie streaming  
* Ticket booking  
* User-generated content  
* Community forums  
* Merchandise sales  
* Live news feeds  
* Third-party account integrations
